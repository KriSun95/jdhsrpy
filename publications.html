NuSTAR Publications

A non-exhaustive list and description of NuSTAR publications that might be relevant to this package.

  1. Grefenstette et al., 2016, ApJ, 826, 20, The First Focused Hard X-ray Images of the Sun with NuSTAR

  2. Hannah et al., 2016, ApJL, 820, L14, The First X-ray Imaging Spectroscopy of Quiescent Solar Active Regions with NuSTAR

  3. Kuhar et al., 2017, ApJ, 835, 6, Evidence of Significant Energy Input in the Late Phase of a Solar Flare from NuSTAR X-ray Observations

  4. Wright et al., 2017, ApJ, 844, 132, Microflare Heating of a Solar Active Region Observed with NuSTAR, Hinode/XRT, and SDO/AIA

  5. Glesener et al., 2017, ApJ, 845, 122, NuSTAR Hard X-Ray Observation of a Sub-A Class Solar Flare

  6. Marsh et al., 2017, ApJ, 849, 131, First NuSTAR Limits on Quiet Sun Hard X-Ray Transient Events

  7. Kuhar et al., 2018, ApJL, 856, L32, NuSTAR Detection of X-Ray Heating Events in the Quiet Sun

  8. Marsh et al., 2018, ApJ, 864, 5, Hard X-Ray Constraints on Small-scale Coronal Heating Events

  9. Hannah et al., 2019, ApJ, 881, 109, Joint X-Ray, EUV, and UV Observations of a Small Microflare

  10. Glesener et al., 2020, ApJL, 891, L34, Accelerated Electrons Observed Down to <7 keV in a NuSTAR Solar Microflare

  11. Cooper et al,. 2020, ApJL, 893, L40, NuSTAR Observation of a Minuscule Microflare in a Solar Active Region

  12. Duncan et al., 2021, ApJ, 908, 29, NuSTAR Observation of Energy Release in 11 Solar Microflares

    • First descriptions of a pile-up correction.

  13. Cooper et al., 2021, MNRAS, 507, 3, NuSTAR Observations of a Repeatedly Microflaring Active Region

  14. Paterson et al., 2023, SolPhys, 298, 47, The First Survey of Quiet Sun Features Observed in Hard X-Rays with NuSTAR

  15. Polito et al., 2023, FrASS, 10, Multi-wavelength observations and modeling of a microflare: constraining non-thermal particle acceleration

  16. Cooper et al., 2024, MNRAS, 529, 1, Detecting non-thermal emission in a solar microflare using nested sampling

    • First description of using nested sampling for spectral model selection.

  17. Paterson et al., 2024, MNRAS, 528, 4, Time evolution of X-ray bright points observed with NuSTAR

  18. Duncan et al., 2024, ApJ, 966, 197, Thermal Evolution of an Active Region Through Quiet and Flaring Phases as Observed by NuSTAR, XRT, and AIA

  19. Bajnokova et al., 2024, MNRAS, 533, 3, First joint X-ray solar microflare observations with NuSTAR and Solar Orbiter/STIX

  20. Ruz et al., 2025, PRL, 135, 141001, NuSTAR as an Axion Helioscope

  21. Bajnoková et al., 2025, ApJL, 992, L1, Observations of a Faint Nonthermal Onset before a GOES C-class Flare

  22. Paterson et al., 2026, MNRAS, 546, 4, Quiet Sun impulsive events observed with NuSTAR during solar minimum

  23. Masek et al., 2026, ApJ, 1010, 130, A Statistical Survey of Faint Solar X-Ray Transients Observed by NuSTAR
